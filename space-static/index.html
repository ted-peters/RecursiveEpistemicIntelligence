What this shows

The original REI-1.0 Python graph logic is running locally in your browser—not a prerecorded chart.

The supporting and opposing sources and all approvals are simulated. Their presence records a fictional review decision, not factual truth.

No trained AI is deployed here. A budget-matched comparison with conventional language models is still outstanding.

How it works

Four ways to handle evidence

NEITHERNo admissible evidence on either side
SUPPORT_ONLYCurrently admitted support, not proof
ATTACK_ONLYCurrently admitted opposition, not disproof
BOTHSupport and opposition coexist; conflict preserved

Source grouping prevents copied reports from being counted repeatedly, provided their shared origin is recorded. Every stage builds a graph and verifies that its event history can be replayed.
